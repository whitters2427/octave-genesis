Session 1 of an ongoing series

House Visioning — Run of Show

The intention of today's session is to clarify a shared sense of the house's future and identify where our preferences may diverge. We'll start with a grounding exercise, sketch our intentions across a set of spectrums, and map our community stakeholder groups and event intentions.

Whatever comes up along the way — agreement, tension, something unresolved — is useful material, not a problem to smooth over before we close. We're not trying to land everything in ninety minutes; today is about making intentional space for the momentum already building in this house, and letting it keep growing over the rest of the series.

Reflective Generative
0:00–0:05

Opening

5 min

Set the frame before the room goes inward.

Script & details

Name the intention out loud: this is the first of a series, today is for generating, session two is for confirming.

Say plainly that there's no wrong answer in what's coming next. This group hasn't done formal values work before, so the permission matters more than it would for a practiced group.

0:05–0:20

Grounding visualization

15 min

Five years from now, each person's own quiet moment reveals the house at its best — felt, then shared.

Script & details

Eyes closed if comfortable. Walk the group through: it's five years from now. Picture your favorite time of day for reflection — whatever that looks like for you. The night before bed. A quiet morning before the house wakes up. An afternoon cup of tea. Walking in through the door after a satisfying day at work or a fun adventure. Settle into that moment.

  • What led you here? Let your mind drift back over the day, or the stretch of time, that brought you to this feeling.
  • What's happening in your chest, your shoulders, your stomach?
  • From this moment, what does the house feel like — in this best-state version of itself?

This best state is each person's own, not a shared scene everyone is picturing together. Let that felt sense rise without naming it yet. Sit with it for a few breaths, around five minutes in, before moving to sharing.

Then go around, about two and a half minutes each, timed. Each person shares what they felt in their body and what emotion surfaced, not yet what they think should change. Sensory language over analysis.

Go in the rotation yourself rather than last, so the room isn't waiting to hear the synthesis before it speaks.

0:20–0:40

Spectrum walk

20 min

Six spectrums, walked and scored on a line on the floor.

Script & details

Mark a line on the ground, one to ten, center as equal. For each spectrum: read it aloud, everyone walks to their number, each person states their number and a short qualifier for why, you tally the four numbers and call out the average before moving to the next.

  • Intimacy ↔ Community
  • Aesthetic ↔ Authentic
  • Practical ↔ Dreamlike
  • Democratic ↔ Decisive
  • Permeability ↔ Sanctuary
  • Structure ↔ Spontaneity

Keep qualifiers to a phrase, not a story, and say your own number and score out loud for the recording. Resist debating any single spectrum; a real split is worth flagging for session two, not resolving on the spot.

Running the companion spectrum tool? Enter each number as it's called out and it tallies the average live — see the sidebar.

0:40–0:50

Stakeholder confirmation

10 min

A fast check on the categories, not a deep map.

Script & details

Read the eight categories aloud and ask if the list feels complete, or if anything should split or merge.

  • Owners
  • Long-term residents
  • Short-term residents
  • Long-term guests (1+ week)
  • Short-stay guests (under 1 week)
  • Personal guests
  • Event guests
  • Frequent, trusted guests (for example, Eva and Matthias)

Stay at the category level. Anything about specific individuals or unresolved gaps goes to a parking-lot note for session two.

Running the companion stakeholder map? It arranges these same eight by closeness rather than as a flat list — see the sidebar.

0:50–1:20

Surface area intentions

30 min

Mostly Subspace, with a nod to House — a quarterly budget to force priorities, then how residents are woven in.

Script & details
12 min — sort 10 min — targets 8 min — residents

Say this before the room starts brainstorming: community is inherent to communal living, and staying connected to community requires permeability. The real question for this block is what surface area we want to open up — what experiences do we want to call in?

Categories & sorting (12 min)

Split the brainstorm into two tracks from the start, since they carry different constraints. No whiteboard assumed this time — post-its on a table or the floor work well for sorting and clustering, or use the digital category board. Give House a quick nod, since it's just two categories; spend most of this time on Subspace, where there's real sorting to do.

House track — community, no revenue
Intimate — owners & residents Gregarious — invited friends & guests
Subspace track — revenue, downstairs

Eight categories, already narrowed down. The room's job is sorting and target-setting, not more trimming.

Nightlife Live music Interactive art Mindful movement Wellness & spirituality Culinary pop-ups / supper clubs Salons & discourse Skillshares & workshops
📋
Literal takeaway: a grid the group walks out with.
Track  →  Category  →  Example event(s)  →  Target / quarter
Sketch these four columns on paper or post-its, or capture them straight into the digital category board — no whiteboard needed.

Target-setting by budget (10 min)

House (2 categories): each person states a number for Intimate gatherings and a number for Gregarious ones per quarter; tally the four and call out the average for each.

Subspace (8 categories): skip one-category-at-a-time debate — it inflates the wish list. Instead, give everyone a fixed quarterly budget (start at 10 total Subspace event slots) to silently split across categories on paper or post-its. Go around, each person calls out their allocation per category, tally and average each column.

Reality check: sum the Subspace averages and say the total out loud against what the group already knows it can sustain, given the setup and ticketing lift. Note the gap; don't resolve it here.

Resident inclusion & comms (8 min)

Say directly: residents haven't always felt in the loop about events, the revenue-share model has felt debilitating relative to the effort involved, and expectations around participation have stayed unspoken. This is for naming that, not fixing it. Generate intentions for how residents get opted into events and what comms touchpoints let them express preferences, make asks, or offer inspiration.

Setup and ticketing logistics get a brief mention here as a known heavy lift, then get parked for a future session.

Running the companion category tool? Use it alongside the post-its, or instead of them — see the sidebar.

1:20–1:30

Synthesis & close

10 min

Each person shares a reflection, a next step, and an ask — then name what's parked.

Script & details

First eight minutes: go around, each person shares three things, kept brief and without debate:

  • A reflection on what came up today
  • Any next steps or intentions this raised for them
  • Anything they'd like to ask for, from you or from the group

This is where you can add your own synthesis-level observations across what came up, in your own turn rather than as commentary on everyone else's.

Last two minutes: name what's parked for later, thank the group, end on time.

Companion tools

  • Spectrum walk tallier — enter each person's number live, watch the average land.
  • Stakeholder proximity map — drag categories closer or further from the core, rather than a flat list.
  • Event category board — sort, cluster, and set quarterly targets for both tracks.

Materials

  • Tape or chalk for the number line on the floor
  • Post-its and a table or clear floor space for sorting event categories — no whiteboard assumed
  • Paper or the digital category board for the final grid: Track / Category / Example / Target per quarter
  • Phone or recorder running for the Notion transcription
  • Something to tally spectrum and budget numbers as they're called out (paper or the companion tools)
  • Visible timer for the room

Facilitator notes

  • You're holding space and participating. Share your own placement and vision in the rounds; save synthesis and steering comments for the close.
  • Say numbers and decisions out loud for the recording. Photograph the stakeholder map, spectrum line, and post-it clusters before you strike them, since transcription won't capture any of it.
  • Six segments in ninety minutes still leaves little slack, especially the thirty-minute surface area block. Keep the timer visible and move briskly between blocks.
  • The Subspace budget is silent and simultaneous on purpose — it forces prioritization instead of inflating the wish list through open debate.
  • The resident and revenue segment is for naming, not solving. Say that before you start it.

Parked for later

  • Session 2: confirm the values, spectrum results, and event intentions generated today; begin shaping actual calendar events.
  • A future session: design the event runbook by event type, work out setup and ticketing logistics, and revisit the revenue-share model with resident input.
  • A future session: promotional and comms channels (word of mouth, online platforms, local businesses), plus brand partnerships and sponsors.
